Brakes & Suspension Overhaul

Uncompromised stopping distance and vehicle balance. We inspect pad thickness, rotor runout, brake fluid hygroscopic moisture levels, strut dampening, control arm bushes, and tie-rod ends using calibrated dial indicators.

  • Low-dust ceramic brake pads & anti-squeal hardware kits
  • Precision on-car rotor micrometer measurement & disc skimming
  • Active adaptive suspension calibration & polyurethane bushes
Powertrain Analytics

Advanced Engine Diagnostics & Repair

Pinpoint precision instead of guesswork. Our master technicians utilise factory diagnostic scan tools to read live PID telemetry data, perform cylinder balance tests, evaluate ignition waveforms, and trace complex drivability issues.

Turnaround: Same-Day to 48 Hrs Full Digital WhatsApp / PDF Report
  • Check engine light DTC code retrieval & module adaptation
  • Timing chain, variable valve timing (VVT) solenoids & camshaft gears
  • Smoke machine leak testing for vacuum and evaporative systems
Preventive Care

Synthetic Fluid & Filter Protection

Protect internal tolerances from heat and friction. We drain and replenish engine oils with exact OEM factory specification viscosities (0W-20, 5W-30, 5W-40), replace high-filtration oil filters, and verify all vital fluid reserves.

Turnaround: 45–60 Minutes Free 25-Point Digital Health Check
  • High-capacity synthetic motor oil & OEM canister filter
  • Cooling system anti-freeze hydrometer test & brake fluid boil check
  • Tyre tread depth, pressure adjustment & underbody visual inspection
Vehicle Electronics

CAN-Bus Networks & Battery Systems

Complete electrical circuit integrity. Modern vehicles feature up to 70 microcomputers communicating over multiplex networks. We diagnose starting issues, high-voltage hybrid batteries, alternator diodes, and electronic steering modules.

Turnaround: 1–3 Hours Warranty: 24–36 Mo Battery Replacement
  • Conductance battery load testing & CCA state-of-health analysis
  • Parasitic battery draw isolation using millivolt fuse drop tests
  • ADAS camera, radar, and parking sensor recalibration
